Due Date vs Planned Date

Separate deadlines from study days

Traditional to-do apps cluster tasks on submission day. DeadlineDeck records when work must be delivered separately from when you plan to do the study.

Capacity Modeling

Real hours, not abstract points

Configure default study capacity and date-specific overrides. If laboratory classes or travel reduce your available time, the model adjusts automatically.
